THE OPERATION

  1. Blind the coast. Take out the radar. Rockets are good against emplacements.
  2. Bring the brains. Clear three guards, then hover over the four engineers. Hold the winch until everyone is aboard.
  3. Break the storm. Two power nodes protect the missile battery. Drop both, then hit the launcher. You have five minutes.
  4. Make it home. Return to the carrier. Hold land over the H to extract.
A GOOD PILOT KNOWS WHEN TO HOVER.

Green pads replenish fuel, armor and all three weapons. Supplies are unlimited. Release the flight control and hold E / WINCH to service.

ON TOUCHSCREENS: left thumb flies, right thumb fires. Tap the weapon tiles to switch. Hold WINCH above a rescue or supply pad. Auto targeting follows the helicopter’s nose; keep FIRE held to strafe around a target. Landscape gives the best view.
